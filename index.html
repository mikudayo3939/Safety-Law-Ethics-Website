Standard 5.3.4 • ETHICS What Developers Should Do

Computing Ethics & Moral Responsibility

Ethics involves moral principles that guide software engineers even when no specific statute forbids an action. Ethical cloud software respects human autonomy and minimizes harm.

  • ▸ Data Minimization: Collecting only approximate location (city-level) if pinpoint GPS isn't required for the app's core function.
  • ▸ Avoiding Dark Patterns: Making "Deny Location" just as easy to tap as "Allow Always."
  • ▸ Confidentiality & NDAs: Developers sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) to protect proprietary algorithms and sensitive user datasets from unauthorized leaks.
Standard 5.3.5 • LAW (U.S. FEDERAL) What Developers Must Do

Federal Laws & Enforceable Regulations

Laws are binding rules enforced by government agencies with financial penalties and legal injunctions. In the United States (Federal Jurisdiction), two key statutes govern location tracking:

  • ▸ FTC Act Section 5 (15 U.S.C. § 45): Prohibits "unfair or deceptive acts." Secretly selling user GPS history after claiming data is "private" is illegal deception.
  • ▸ COPPA (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506): Strictly requires verifiable parental consent before collecting precise geolocation data from children under age 13.
  • ▸ Data Security Mandates: Storing unencrypted sensitive location logs in the cloud that expose users to stalking or identity theft triggers federal enforcement.

Jurisdiction: United States (Federal)

Credible Sources & Legal Authorities

All legal standards referenced in this learning module are governed under United States Federal Jurisdiction and enforced by the Federal Trade Commission (FTC).

  • 1. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Act, Section 5 (15 U.S.C. § 45): Official enforcement guidance on geolocation privacy and unfair/deceptive data broker practices. ftc.gov/privacy-security ↗
  • 2. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506 & 16 CFR Part 312: Federal statutory rules requiring verifiable parental consent prior to collecting precise geolocation information from children under 13. ftc.gov/coppa-rule ↗
  • 3. ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct (Section 1.6 – Respect Privacy): Computing industry standard establishing ethical obligations for data minimization and user consent. acm.org/code-of-ethics ↗
